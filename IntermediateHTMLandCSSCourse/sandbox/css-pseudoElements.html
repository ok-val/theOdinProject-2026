Pseudo-elements are less specific than pseudo-classes. They have two colons rather than one

Here are few common types:

  • ::marker -- selects the bullet markers of li types
  • ::first-letter and ::first-line -- selects, respectively, the first letter and first line of some text
  • ::seelction -- selects the highlighting behavior when users select some texts
  • ::before and ::after -- selects, respectively, what comes before and after chosen elements. Quite handy for decorating texts with unicodes (remember to spec the unicode-range if you do)
