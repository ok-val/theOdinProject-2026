## What is client-side validation, really?

> [!definition] Client-side validation 
> **Client-side validation is an initial check on the client side.** 
> It's important for good UX. It simply makes the work easier for user. Users can see errors and fix straight away.
> Client validation is done on client browser, server validation is done on server. (Duh!)
> It serves as half of the security measure when it comes to validation. It must always be paired with server-side validation.

## Why do we insist on validating form? 

* ~={blue}Right data, right format, right place:=~ Crap in, crap out mentality
* ~={blue}Protect users' data:=~ Forcing users to enter secure passwords protects their info
* ~={blue}Protect ourselves:=~ From malicious users looking to damage our servers
