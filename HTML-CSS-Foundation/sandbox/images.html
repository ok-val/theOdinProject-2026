Types of images

There are four main types of images used on the Internet:

  • JPG: Good for photos and gradients with large color palettes. No transparency
  • GIF: Good for simple animations, but with limited color palette. No transparency
  • PNG: Good for pixel-based icons, diagrams, logos, but need transparency handling, making them heavier than JPG.
  • SVG: Good for vector-based graphics, icons, logos. Vector-based scales infinitely. Only downside is one needs to take time to create them.
