Collapsing Margins

Example text.

Explanation

Depending on whether two elements whose margins touch have positive or negative margins, the results will be different:

  • Two positive margins will collapse into the largest value of the two.
  • Two negative margins will collapse into the smallest.
  • One negative and one positive margin will collapse into the sum of both.

Purpose

Simple: Consider a header like h3 that would be used for multiple pages.

Now consider that following this h3 can be any element from a p to a custom div. These elements could just have different margin-tops. Adding h3's margin-bottom to these different margin-tops would ultimately cause too may variations.

Consider this a blessing :)
