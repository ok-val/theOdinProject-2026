Omurice

Description

The rice tastes sweet and savory and the eggs are creamy and buttery. It's a popular comfort food at home and diner restaurants, particularly popular among children. Read more here

Ingredients

  • Shirataki rice (or a day-old cooked white rice)
  • Ghee (or butter)
  • Olive oil
  • Yellow onion
  • Turkey bacon (or cooked ham, chicken, or Canadian bacon)
  • Frozen peas and carrots
  • Salt and pepper
  • Ketchup
  • Eggs
  • Dairy-free creamer
