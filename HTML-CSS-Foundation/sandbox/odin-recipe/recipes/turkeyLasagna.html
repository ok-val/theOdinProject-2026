Description

Bake up this Quick & Easy Turkey Lasagna recipe with lean, sweet Italian turkey sausage and three kinds of cheese. Full of flavor, done in under 60 minutes, and super kid-friendly! Read more here.

Ingredients

  • 19.5-ounce of sweet Italian turkey sausage
  • 26-ounce spaghetti sauce
  • no-boil lasagna noodles
  • 15-ounce container ricotta cheese
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese, divided
